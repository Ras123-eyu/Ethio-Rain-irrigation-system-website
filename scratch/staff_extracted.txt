About Us
Ethio Rain Irrigation System PLC is an Ethiopian company engaged in
surveying, study, design, manufacturing, consulting, supply and
installation of comprehensive irrigation & water supply systems,
maintenance and after sales services. The company was established in
2003 E.C to set up the most recent and advanced center pivot
Technology Company in Ethiopia.
Our company has taken different trainings in South Africa on
operation, manufacturing process, installation and maintenance of
center pivot systems and water management. We have local experience
in supervision and installation of meki Irrigation projects 1000 ha 15
pivots and 7 pivots in shallo irrigation project
